# MirexCargo

> **Manage. Move. Track.**

MirexCargo is a complete logistics management platform designed for freight
forwarders, logistics companies, transport operations, warehouse teams,
customs/documentation teams, finance teams and management.

The platform combines CRM, TMS, freight forwarding, shipment tracking,
documentation, warehouse, transport, finance, reporting and operational
workflows into a unified system.

---

## 1. Product Scope

MirexCargo covers the following major business areas:

- CRM
- Sales
- Quotations
- Booking
- Air Freight
- Ocean Freight
- Land Transport
- Courier
- Customs & Clearance
- Documentation
- Warehouse
- Transport
- Shipment Tracking
- Finance
- Tasks & Alerts
- Reports
- Masters
- HRMS
- Settings
- Public Shipment Tracking
